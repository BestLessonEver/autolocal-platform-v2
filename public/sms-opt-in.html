📱 SMS Opt-In Flow — AutoLocal.ai

This page documents the SMS consent collection process on autolocal.ai.

Order Form with SMS Consent

Located at autolocal.ai/#order — the "Get Your Free Custom Website" section:

I agree to receive text messages from AutoLocal.ai about my website status and updates. Message frequency varies, max 10/mo. Reply STOP to unsubscribe. Msg & data rates may apply. Consent is not a condition of purchase. Privacy Policy · Terms
↑

Checkbox is unchecked by default. User must actively check it to opt in.

Key compliance details:

• Checkbox is unchecked by default — requires active user consent

• Phone number field is optional — not required for website generation

• Consent text includes: brand name, message frequency, STOP instructions, data rates disclaimer

• Links to Privacy Policy and Terms

• Consent is explicitly stated as not a condition of purchase

Message Types Sent

  • Website preview ready notifications
  • Hosting activation confirmations
  • Domain registration updates
  • Account and billing notifications

Opt-Out

Users can reply STOP at any time to unsubscribe from all messages. Reply HELP for support information.
